Common questions

My family isn't getting notifications

On the phone that receives check-ins, open iOS Settings → Notifications → Pruuf and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open Pruuf once so it can register with Apple.

How do I change my check-in time?

Tap the gear in the top-right corner, then "Time and name".

How do I add someone?

Tap the gear, then "My loved ones", then "Add a loved one". Pick them from your contacts and Pruuf writes the invitation text for you. They install Pruuf, choose "I get check-ins", and enter your 6-character code.

What happens if I travel to another time zone?

Nothing for you to do. Pruuf follows your phone's time zone automatically. Your family sees both your local time and their own.

How do I delete my account?

Tap the gear, then "Delete my account". This permanently removes your profile, check-in history and all connections. You can also email us and we'll do it within 30 days.
