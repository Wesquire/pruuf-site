Pruuf

Peace of mind, one tap a day.

Pruuf is the simplest way to tell the people who love you that you're OK. Open the app, tap one big button, and everyone you've added gets a notification. If you don't tap it by your chosen time, they're alerted automatically.

Pruuf is not an emergency service. It notifies the family members you have added — it does not contact emergency services or any monitoring centre.
