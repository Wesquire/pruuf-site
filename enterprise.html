What you are actually buying

Three things, not an app.

01

A presence layer

The cheapest possible daily confirmation that a person is alive and functioning: one tap, on the phone or watch they already own.

No pendant, no installation visit, and — the part that decides adoption — no stigma. “I’m not old enough for that” is what kills pendant programmes. Nobody has ever refused an app for being demeaning.

02

An escalation graph

When a day is missed, the family is notified and so are you. Whoever responds first presses “I’ve got this”, and everyone else stands down.

Your on-call coordinator is a node in that graph, not an afterthought — which means your organisation finds out at the same moment the family does, rather than from an angry phone call two days later.

03

An audit trail you can prove

Every alert and every response is timestamped and kept — and every check-in is cryptographically signed by the client’s own device, then verified before it is recorded.

“The family was notified at 10:07. Sarah acknowledged at 10:11. The caregiver arrived at 10:34.” That sentence closes deals and defuses liability conversations, and it is the reason the record exists at all.

The signature is what makes it more than our word. A verified check-in could not have been produced by anybody without that unlocked device — us included. How it works →

The comparison you’ll be asked about

The anti-pendant.

Personal emergency response is a $1.5–2 billion US market built on hardware, contracts and monitoring centres. It works. It is also expensive, requires an installation, and a large share of the people it is bought for refuse to wear it.

Pruuf does less on purpose, at a price that lets you put it on the entire roster instead of on the six clients who agreed.

Rollout

The hardest step is the one you already do.

Getting a 78-year-old set up is the step where consumer software loses most of its users. In your business, a member of staff does it standing next to them, on a visit that was already scheduled.

The weakest link in the consumer funnel becomes a three-minute task your caregivers perform. That is the whole reason this works at scale for you and not for a stranger on the internet.

Who this is built for

Three kinds of operator.

Private-duty home care

You sell hours of human presence and cannot hire enough humans. Between visits — often six days a week — you have no eyes on the client at all.

Pruuf gives you daily coverage without a minute of labour, and gives your sales conversation a sentence nobody else has: “we check on your mother every day, including the days we’re not there.”

Franchise care networks

Every office is independently owned, but the brand can hand one tool to the whole network with a pre-negotiated rate.

Four hundred offices at a hundred clients each is 40,000 protected people from a single relationship — and a differentiator the network owns rather than rents.

Independent & senior living

Independent living is sold on the promise that nobody checks on residents. That promise is also the operator’s worst liability.

A daily check-in preserves the independence branding, gives the front desk a “not yet today” list, and turns family visibility into an amenity you can market.

Data and security

What we hold, and what we don’t claim.

  • Very little is stored. A first name, a check-in time, who is connected to whom, and the timestamps of check-ins and alerts.
  • No location, ever. The app does not request location permission and holds no location data about anyone.
  • No health records. Pruuf is not a clinical system and deliberately holds nothing that would make it one.
  • Row-level isolation. Every read is scoped to the organisation or person making it, enforced in the database itself rather than by application code.
  • Deletion on request, for a client or for your whole organisation, and it removes the records rather than hiding them.

We do not currently hold SOC 2, HIPAA attestation or any other certification, and we are not going to pretend otherwise on a website in order to get into a procurement process. If your review requires one, tell us early and we will tell you honestly where we are. Pruuf holds no protected health information by design, which is usually the shortest route through that conversation.
